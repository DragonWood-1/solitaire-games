Game Guides & Strategy

Most people assume solitaire is a game of luck. It is not — or at least, not entirely. Roughly four out of five Klondike deals can be won with perfect play, yet the typical player wins a far smaller share of them. That gap is not luck. It is technique, and technique can be learned.

The same holds across every game on this site. Sudoku has a well-defined ladder of solving methods that carries you from simple scanning all the way to X-Wings and swordfish. Chess has a handful of opening principles that quietly prevent most beginner catastrophes. Mahjong solitaire rewards a specific discipline about which layer to clear first. Poker rests on arithmetic that takes an afternoon to learn and a lifetime to apply well.

These guides are written to teach that technique concretely. No filler, no vague encouragement to "plan ahead" — just the specific decisions that change outcomes.

Where to Start

If you are new to solitaire, read the rules guide first, then the strategy guide. The single change that lifts most players' win rates is learning when not to move a card to the foundation — it is counterintuitive, it is explained in detail there, and it costs nothing to try on your next game.

If you already know the rules and simply want to win more often, skip straight to the strategy guide.
